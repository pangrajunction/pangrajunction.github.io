Who We Are

More than a café.
It's a vibe.

Pangra Junction is Kathmandu's most unique hangout spot — where tire-inspired aesthetics meet comfort food and warm chiya. Our seats are literally tire-inspired, giving the whole place a raw, cozy, industrial character you won't find anywhere else.

Whether you're here for a quick breakfast, a long evening with friends over hookah, or just a meaningful break — Pangra Junction is your spot.
