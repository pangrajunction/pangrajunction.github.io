Pangra Junction

A neon-lit street café in Kathmandu where tire culture meets bold food, strong tea, late-night snacks, and pure hangout energy.

What is Pangra Junction?

Pangra Junction is a tire-inspired street café in Kathmandu built for everyday chaos, comfort, and connection. It’s not a fine-dining place — it’s a hangout spot.

People come here for:
  • 🔥 hot chiya and quick bites
  • 🛞 tire-seat street vibe seating
  • 🍜 momo, snacks, and late-night food
  • ⚡ chill conversations and long stays

The identity is simple: raw street energy + warm food + neon personality.
